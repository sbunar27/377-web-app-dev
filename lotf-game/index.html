Survival Protocol

1. The Sanity Meter: You start with 100% Sanity. Each incorrect answer decays your Sanity by 15%. If your Sanity drops to 0%, the Beast consumes you and it is Game Over.

2. The Assembly (Lifelines): You have 3 sacred tribal items to assist you. Each item can only be used once:

  • The Conch Shell: Invokes democratic order. Temporarily removes 2 incorrect choices.
  • Piggy's Glasses: Directs absolute scientific focus. Reveals the correct answer, but fractures your sight (costs 10 Sanity as standard fatigue).
  • The Lord of the Flies: Whispers chaotic truth. Reveals a cryptic hint directly from the Beast.

3. Victory: Complete all 15 survival trials to salvage rescue. Your final archetype depends on your performance.
